Backup & restore

Your data lives only in this browser, and phones quietly clear storage for apps you haven't opened in a while — a backup file is the only thing that survives that. Save writes everything (sessions, runs, weights, goals, plans & notes) to a file; Send hands it to your phone's share sheet so it can go straight to Drive or your mail. A backup sitting in your phone's Downloads dies with the phone — get it off the device. Restore brings it back here or on a new one.
